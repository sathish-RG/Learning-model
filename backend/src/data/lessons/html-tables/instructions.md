## Your mission: build a weekly timetable

Build an accessible timetable showing what you'll study each weekday.

1. **The table and its title.** Under the `<h1>`, add a `<table>` with the `<caption>` `My Coding Week`.
2. **The head.** Add a `<thead>` with one row of three `<th scope="col">` cells: `Day`, `Morning`, `Afternoon`.
3. **The body.** Add a `<tbody>` with five rows, Monday to Friday. The first cell of each row is a `<th scope="row">` with the day; the other two are `<td>` cells with what you'll study (e.g. `HTML`, `Practice`).
4. **Merge a cell.** On Wednesday, replace the morning and afternoon cells with one cell that says `Project day` and spans both columns.
5. **The feet.** Add a `<tfoot>` with one row: a `<th scope="row">` that says `Total`, and one cell spanning the other two columns that says `20 hours`.

✅ **You're done when** the preview shows a captioned table with 5 days, Wednesday's `Project day` stretching across two columns, and a total row at the bottom.
