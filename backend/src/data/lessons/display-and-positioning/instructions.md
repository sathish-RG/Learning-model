## Your mission: badges, a sticky header and a floating button

The page is locked; use only `display`, `position`, the offset properties (`top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`) and `z-index` in `styles.css`.

1. **Tags in a row.** Make the items in `.tags` sit side by side with `display: inline-block`.
2. **Hide the draft.** Hide `.draft-note` completely.
3. **A sticky header.** Make `.site-header` stick to the top of the screen while you scroll (`top: 0`). Give it `z-index: 10` so it stays above the cards.
4. **Badges on the corners.** Make each `.card` an anchor, then pin its `.badge` to the card's top-right corner, sticking out a little: `top: -10px` and `right: -10px`.
5. **A floating help button.** Fix `.help-button` to the screen, `20px` from the bottom and `20px` from the right.

✅ **You're done when** the tags sit in a row, the draft note is gone, every "NEW" badge hangs off its own card's corner, and the header and the `?` button stay on screen while you scroll the preview.
