Coffee 101

Everything a beginner needs to know about the world's favourite morning drink.

Where it comes from

Coffee beans are the seeds of a bright red fruit called a coffee cherry. The cherries grow on bushes in warm countries close to the equator, like Brazil, Ethiopia, Colombia and India.

Roasting

Fresh coffee beans are green and taste grassy. Roasting turns them brown and creates hundreds of new flavours. Light roasts taste fruity; dark roasts taste bold and chocolatey.

"Coffee is a language in itself."

Brewing at home

Start with freshly ground beans and water just off the boil. Use about one tablespoon of coffee for every cup of water, then adjust until it tastes right to you.
